Talekeep — Support

Talekeep is a local-first app that archives your chats into an encrypted library on your own Mac, and lets you browse, search, and ask AI about them — privately.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email enquiry@craveva.com and we’ll get back to you.

Common questions

Where is my data? On your Mac only, in a folder you choose, encrypted at rest. Talekeep has no servers and no account.

I forgot my passphrase. If you chose passphrase protection, use your saved recovery code. Without either, the archive can’t be recovered — by design, because the key is never sent to us.

How do I connect Telegram? You provide your own Telegram api_id / api_hash (from my.telegram.org) and sign in; credentials are stored in your macOS Keychain and sent only to Telegram.
