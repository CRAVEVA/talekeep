FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your Mac, in a folder you choose (or the app's own container by default). Message text is encrypted at rest. Nothing is uploaded to us — Talekeep operates no servers.

Can Talekeep (the developer) read my messages?

No. There is no account, no telemetry, and no server. Your archive never leaves your device unless you copy it.

Why do I need my own Telegram api_id?

Talekeep connects as your own Telegram application — nothing is proxied through us. It takes ~2 minutes at my.telegram.org and the app walks you through it.

Does it support WhatsApp / Signal / ChatGPT?

Telegram is supported today. AI-chat import (ChatGPT, Claude) and more messengers are on the roadmap above — Plus subscribers get new connectors as they ship.

What does “ask any AI” mean?

By default, Ask AI uses Apple's built-in model running directly on your Mac — it works offline and your messages never leave your machine. You can instead bring your own API key (a cloud service, or a local model server you run), or enable the read-only MCP server so AI apps like Claude Desktop can search your archive — each option behind explicit consent, active only while you keep it on.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the archive folder. That's it — it's a file on your disk, not an account.
